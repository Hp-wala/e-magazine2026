About us

The English Department of Pub Kamrup College has long been a hub of literature, culture, and critical thought. Through teaching and creativity, the department nurtures a love for language, expression, and imagination.

Our goal is to provide a platform where creativity meets insight, and where every contribution becomes a part of our shared journey in literature and life.

As part of the English Department of Pub Kamrup College, this e-magazine serves as a collective space where students and teachers share their voices through literature, culture, and critical thought, keeping alive the spirit of creativity and expression.
