About us

The English Department of Pub Kamrup College has long been a hub of literature, culture, and critical thought. Through teaching and creativity, the department nurtures a love for language, expression, and imagination.

Our goal is to provide a platform where creativity meets insight, and where every contribution becomes a part of our shared journey in literature and life.

As part of the English Department of Pub Kamrup College, this e-magazine serves as a collective space where students and teachers share their voices through literature, culture, and critical thought, keeping alive the spirit of creativity and expression.

Whispers of Poetry

“Poetry is the spontaneous overflow of powerful feelings arrising out of emotions recollected in tranquility” – Wordsworth.

Whispers of Poetry is a creative corner where words bloom with rhythm and grace. Here, students of Pub Kamrup College let their voices wander through verses of love, longing, and life. Inspired by the eternal echoes of Romantic poets, this space captures imagination, emotion, and dreams that speak gently to the soul.

Echoes of Thought

“Shall I compare thee to a summer's day? Thou art more lovely and more temperate.” – William Shakespeare

Echoes of Thought is a literary corner where the voices of English Department student's merge with the timeless brilliance of poets like Shakespeare, Keats, and Wordsworth. Through essays, stories, and reflections, imagination takes flight, emotions find expression, and thoughts weave together, creating a tapestry that speaks to every heart.
